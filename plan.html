For grown-ups: loan jar & monthly details

We mow April–October. Each business year runs April–March. Money goes into a separate loan jar during mowing season, then pays the loan every month, including winter. Moving money into the jar is not a payment. The blue line only counts money actually paid back.

Swipe the table sideways to see all the columns.

Giving and saving are shares of revenue. “Spend” means money available after giving, saving, and loan-jar transfers. Loan proceeds buy the mower and are not revenue. Monthly income is averaged across 7 mowing months; dollars are rounded on the graphs. Loan interest is calculated monthly and the last payment clears the balance exactly. Age labels mean age at the start of each mowing season.

Both choices use the same price, cut count, and giving/saving percentages. If extra family help is needed, the payoff forecast assumes it is supplied separately from mowing income.
